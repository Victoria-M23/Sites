About Us

Who we are

We are an undergraduate-focused, multidisciplinary design team managing biomedical research and development projects. Since 2018, we have worked with stakeholders at the University of Toronto and nearby hospitals to deliver tailored, high-impact projects, which we present at conferences and nation-wide competitions.

Current Projects

What we're building now

Competition

True North Biomedical Competition

A portable cardiac triage system for natural disasters

Research

Research Paper Team

A review paper on the treatment of Alzheimer’s disease through applications of hydrogel towards restoring neural stem cell functions

Design Team

Alinnovate: H2i OrthoBreathe Design Team

Allinovate’s OrthoBreathe is a nasal strip that alleviates symptoms of allergic rhinitis by trapping humidity in the nasal chamber to keep the mucosal lining hydrated.

Design Team

UTMIST x UTBIOME: MEG2Truth Design Team

An audit and intervention system to evaluate and mitigate MetaAI Brain2Qwerty V1’s reliance on KenLM for neural signal to text decoding accuracy.

Design Team

Wearabales Design Team

A wearable that reads heart and skin signals to detect stress and emotion

Design Team

Microfluidics Design Team @ Farcas Lab

A compact, point-of-care microfluidic diagnostic device to detect microhematuria

Project

Antimicrobial Susceptibility Testing (AST) Project

A microscope that specializes in rapid AST using dark-field microscopy and AI analysis

Design Team

SEDS Lab @ UofT: CPR Feedback Device

Real-time touch and sound feedback to help bystanders deliver CPR

Design Team

UofT NeuroTech × UTBIOME: EEG Elbow Design Team

A brain-controlled elbow brace: Translates EEG signals during executed movement attempts into arm brace movement.

Past Projects

What we've built since 2018

2024 – 2025 · Farcas Lab, St. Michael's Hospital

Controlled Scope-Irrigation Tube

A controlled irrigation system with a sand-filtering unit and surgical control box for removing small kidney stones.

2022 – 2025 · Anahana Wellness

Breathing Biofeedback

A wearable breathing-monitoring patch with biofeedback and data analysis for detecting COPD from HexoSkin sensor data.

2022 – 2024 · Farcas Lab, St. Michael's Hospital

IrriGREAT

An automated bag-switching controller and flow-adjusting system for continuous bladder irrigation.

2021 – 2023 · St. George Physiotherapy Clinic

Back Health Monitoring & Rehabilitation

A back-injury monitoring system using textile-integrated sensors to support rehabilitation exercises.

2021 – 2022

Synthetic Data Generation

Synthetic datasets for biomedical research and computational modelling.

2021 – 2022

Ventilator Design

Respiratory support devices for critical care needs during the COVID-19 pandemic.

2020 – 2021

AI Fall Detection

Machine learning classifiers that detect falls in seniors with high accuracy.

2020 – 2021

Cystic Fibrosis Symptom Alleviation

A low-cost, automated airway-clearing device that loosens mucus for cystic fibrosis patients.

2019 – 2020 · Tetra Society

Accessible Wheelchair Attachments

A wheelchair attachment with a locking pivot and forearm cradle that helps users with arm movement.

2018 – 2020

Prosthetic Leg

A low-cost, modular above-knee prosthetic focused on joint mechanics, for users in developing countries.

2018 – 2020

Stroke Rehabilitation Device

Rehabilitation devices that support recovery after a stroke.

Join the team

We're looking for students who are passionate about science and design to work on research, design and collaboration. Applications usually open at the start of the fall semester, and students from any UofT discipline are welcome. Missed the window? Reach out any time.
